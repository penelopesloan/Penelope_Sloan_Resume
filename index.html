My Story

The entrepreneurial instinct showed up early.

At 7, selling handmade bath bombs and art around the neighbourhood.

At 11, hired at a local coffee shop.

At 17, assistant manager of a restaurant.

At 22, fresh out of design school, Penelope Sloan Creative was born.

Sixteen years later: a global consultancy spanning brand strategy, spatial design, AI systems, and investment intelligence.

The thread that connects it all: turning ambiguous ideas into structured, scalable realities.
